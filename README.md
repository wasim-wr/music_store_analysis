# 🎵 Music Store Analysis Dashboard

A business intelligence dashboard built to analyze music store sales, customer behavior, and artist performance.

---

## 1. Project Objective

Analyze music store data to identify top-performing artists, cities, and customers — helping management make data-driven decisions on sales, marketing, and inventory.

---

## 2. Dataset Overview

| Table | Description |
|---|---|
| Invoice | Customer purchase records by city & country |
| Customer | Customer details including location & spending |
| Track / Album | Song data with length, genre, and artist |
| Employee | Staff hierarchy information |

---

## 3. Process

- Connected and explored raw tables in SQL
- Wrote queries to answer business questions
- Cleaned and aggregated results
- Built interactive dashboard in Power BI

---

## 4. Business Questions & KPIs Solved

| Question | Finding |
|---|---|
| Who is the most senior employee? | **L7 — Mohan Madan** |
| Which country has the most invoices? | **USA** with 131 invoices |
| Which city generates the most revenue? | **Prague** — $273.24 |
| Who is the best customer? | **R Madhav** — $144.54 spent |
| Which rock bands have the most songs? | **Led Zeppelin** (114), **U2** (112) |
| Top spender on Queen? | **Hugh** — $27.72 |

---

## 5. Dashboard Insights

- 🇺🇸 **USA dominates** purchase volume with 561 rock music purchases
- 🏙️ **Prague** is the most profitable city despite not being the largest market
- 🎸 **Led Zeppelin & U2** lead rock band song counts by a significant margin
- 💳 **Czech Republic** produces the highest individual customer spend (R Madhav)
- 🎵 **Rock** is the most purchased genre across all countries

---

## 6. Project Insights

- Customer spending is concentrated in a small group — top 5 customers account for a disproportionate share of revenue
- European cities (Prague, Bordeaux, Montréal) lead invoice value despite lower volume
- Rock genre dominates globally, making it the safest category for inventory and promotions

---

## 7. Final Conclusion

The music store's revenue is driven by a loyal core of high-value customers in European cities, with Rock music leading all genres. Targeting these customers with personalized offers and expanding Rock catalog inventory would directly improve sales.
